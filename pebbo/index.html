Pebbo Pets — Support

Pebbo puts a tiny animated pet in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen.

Start or switch a pet

Open Pebbo and tap a pet. Golden Pup and Maine Coon are free. Paid pets can be unlocked individually, or you can unlock the full roster. Tapping another owned pet switches the current Live Activity without creating a duplicate.

Keep Pebbo active

Turn on Keep Pebbo active in Settings. Pebbo repairs a missing activity whenever the app opens or returns to the foreground, and replaces an aging session before Apple’s eight-hour Dynamic Island limit.

For best-effort returns while Pebbo is closed, review the disclosure and enable Closed-app continuity. All day requests a replacement before the prior session expires; Morning only makes one request near your selected hour. Apple controls delivery and may delay or decline presentation.

If the pet disappears

Open Pebbo, or choose Refresh now in Settings. Swiping away a Live Activity removes that session. To stop the pet and all scheduled returns, use Dismiss in Pebbo.

Appearance and motion

Settings can show or hide the pet name, use Follow iPhone, Light, or Dark for the Lock Screen card, pause motion, and enable an expiry reminder. Follow iPhone refreshes when Pebbo next runs.

Purchases and restoration

Paid pets are one-time, non-consumable purchases, never subscriptions. You can buy one pet or the all-pets option. The App Store shows the localized price before purchase.

To restore, open Settings and tap Restore purchases. If access still does not appear, tap Refresh access, then fully close and reopen Pebbo. Include the full RevenueCat customer ID shown in Settings when contacting support.

Invite friends and earn pets

Tap Invite 2 friends, get a free pet on the pet picker, or Invite friends & earn pets in Settings. Share your invite link or copy your code. Each friend must download Pebbo, open it, and apply your code. Every two friends earns one pet of your choice; four earns two, and so on. A link tap alone does not count.

Your invite page includes Copy code & get Pebbo. After installation, return to that page and tap Open Pebbo & apply invite, or choose Use a friend’s code in Pebbo and paste the code. One friend can credit one inviter once.

When a reward is ready, tap Choose your free pet, select a locked pet, and confirm the unlock. Rewards are saved independently from purchases and have no expiry. Refresh progress checks for recent referrals and retries a pending invite or unlock. Earned pets remain usable offline after they have synced.

Referral rewards use a private identity saved on this iPhone. They do not automatically transfer to another phone through Restore purchases. For help, include your referral code when contacting support.

Ratings

Pebbo uses Apple’s standard review sheet. Apple decides whether it appears. Rate Pebbo in Settings opens the App Store review page.
