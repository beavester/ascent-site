Pebbo Pets — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

The short version

Pebbo Pets collects no data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

What the app does

Pebbo Pets shows an animated pixel pet in your iPhone's Dynamic Island. Your pet choice stays on your device. The app makes no network requests to any server we operate — we don't have one.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat, a purchase-infrastructure provider, to validate purchases and enable restore on a new device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated app identifier and your purchase state — never your name, email, or anything that identifies you. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version ships.
